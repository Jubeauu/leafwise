Leafwise

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Leafwise helps you identify plants, diagnose plant problems and remember to care for your plants. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and the choices you have. In short: there are no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and your plant collection stays on your device.

Who we are

Leafwise is published by Kairia. You can reach us at support@kairia.fr.

Data stored on your device only

Your plants, nicknames, rooms, photos you attach to plants, care history, notes and reminder settings are stored locally on your iPhone. We do not receive or keep a copy. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Photos you scan

When you use Identify or Plant Doctor, the photo you take or choose (resized and compressed) is sent over an encrypted connection to our server, which forwards it to Google's Gemini API to generate the identification or diagnosis. The photo is processed only to return your result. We do not store your scanned photos on our servers and we do not use them to build profiles. Google processes the request as our service provider under the Gemini API terms.

Leafwise only accesses the photos you explicitly choose in the system photo picker, or the photo you take with the camera.

Anonymous usage counter

To apply the free scan limit and fair-use limits for subscribers, our server keeps a counter of scans linked to a random, anonymous app identifier generated on your device. It contains no name, email, phone number or device advertising identifier. It is hosted by Supabase in the European Union.

Subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active, using the same anonymous app identifier and the purchase receipts Apple provides. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Notifications

Watering reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No push notification server is involved.

What we do not do

  • No sign-up, no account, no email collection.
  • No advertising, no third-party analytics, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share your data.

Retention and your rights

The anonymous usage counter is kept only as long as needed to apply scan limits. Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR), you may ask us to access or delete data associated with your anonymous identifier by writing to support@kairia.fr. You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

Children

Leafwise is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
