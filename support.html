Support

Questions, feedback or a plant we got wrong? Write to us at support@kairia.fr. We usually reply within two business days.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Leafwise and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

I paid but Pro is not unlocked

In Leafwise, open Settings and tap Restore purchases, using the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

I don't get watering reminders

Check that notifications are allowed for Leafwise in the Settings app, then pick your preferred reminder time in Leafwise › Settings.

Is the identification always right?

Leafwise uses AI and can make mistakes. For the best result, photograph a single plant in good light, with a clear view of its leaves or flowers. Treatment suggestions are for guidance only.
